# AI Hand-off Blueprint

## 1. Current Objective
Autonomous research-to-trading pipeline with Supabase persistent storage, Lumibot + Alpaca execution, and responsive Vercel-hosted UI. Ensure daily research picks automatically save and sync to Supabase `research_picks` table without date drift, reflect accurately in Deep Research view, and run according to user's Atlantic Time zone (ADT/AST).

## 2. Active Architecture
- **Backend**:
  - `backend/server.py`: FastAPI server exposing `/signals`, `/research`, and trade status.
  - `backend/strategy.py`: Lumibot trading loop (`on_trading_iteration`), Alpaca paper account balance fallbacks, AI conviction grading (`ai_grade >= 55`).
  - `backend/research.py`: Deep research cycle (`run_research_cycle`) + automatic scheduler with explicit ADT time logging.
  - `backend/utils.py`: Timezone mapping (`America/Halifax` ADT/AST vs `US/Eastern` ET), market hours checks (`is_market_open`, `is_research_window`).
  - `backend/state.py`: Absolute path resolution for `reports.json` across root, `backend/`, and `frontend/public/` to prevent stale data drift.
  - `backend/main.py`: Concurrently launches background trading loop, research scheduler, and FastAPI server.
- **Frontend**:
  - `frontend/src/context/AppContext.jsx`: App state hub, syncs backend reports, debounces auto-save to Supabase.
  - `frontend/src/lib/supabase.js`: Supabase client, schema migration scripts, `savePicksToSupabase()` using correct `run_date`, and `fetchPicksFromSupabase()`.
  - `frontend/src/pages/PicksHistoryPage.jsx`: Historical pick explorer grouped by date.
  - `frontend/src/components/ExpandableReportCards.jsx`: Neural Research Reports display for current picks.
  - `frontend/src/index.css`: Responsive TradingView modal, full height/width chart styling on mobile and desktop.
  - `vercel.json` / `frontend/vercel.json`: SPA rewrite configuration resolving 404 on page reload.
  - `frontend/public/reports.json`: Static mirror of current research reports for fallback Vercel presentation.

## 3. Timezone & Schedule Mapping (Atlantic Time)
- **Timezone**: Atlantic Daylight Time (ADT / `America/Halifax`, UTC-3) vs US/Eastern (ET, UTC-4).
- **Pre-Market Research Scan**: 9:30 AM ADT (8:30 AM ET).
- **Market Open**: 10:30 AM ADT (9:30 AM ET).
- **Market Close / Scan End**: 5:00 PM ADT (4:00 PM ET).
- **Run Interval**: Minimum 3-hour cool-down between cycles during active window. Weekends skipped (`weekday >= 5`).
